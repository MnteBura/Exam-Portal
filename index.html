FAMILY SCHOOL PRINCIPLE

Become stronger through structure.

Family School is designed around the idea that students become stronger when their environment helps them learn, focus, plan, practice and reflect.

The goal is not to make students dependent on technology. The goal is to help them develop independence, discipline and confidence.
